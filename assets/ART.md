# Artes e fontes

Referência: `../image.png`, fornecida pelo usuário e preservada sem alterações.

As duas artes foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` da skill `imagegen`, usando a referência original. Não foi usado o fallback CLI/API.

- `apiary.png`: cenário e molduras, 1672 × 941. Os textos, botões, indicadores e personagens são desenhados pelo jogo.
- `sprites.png`: atlas RGBA com transparência, 1254 × 1254.
- `sprites.json`: regiões dos dezesseis sprites; Ruby2D compartilha uma textura e recorta as regiões ao desenhar.
- `ui/*.svg`: painéis e botões vetoriais escritos em código, com bordas e gradientes.
- `fonts/DroidSans.ttf` e `fonts/LiberationSansNarrow-Bold.ttf`: fontes locais para uma aparência consistente. Licenças em `fonts/DroidSans-LICENSE.txt` e `fonts/Liberation-LICENSE.txt`.

## Prompt do cenário

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: static background plate for an actual playable Ruby2D honey farming game. Input image 1 is the edit target. Keep the exact 16:9 full-screen composition, warm polished hand-painted casual game style, lighting, trees, sky, distant cottage, wooden fences, central vertical dirt path and horizontal path, all flowers, honey drips, wooden top HUD frame and parchment sidebar outer frame at their original positions. Remove ALL text, digits, logos and icons everywhere; remove all individual inner HUD stat boxes, progress bar, sidebar cards, tabs and buttons, leaving a continuous dark wooden empty top HUD and a blank light cream parchment area inside the right sidebar. Keep the empty wooden location sign at upper left and the empty small signpost at center path. Remove ALL beehives including roofs and numbered plaques, remove the beekeeper character, all bees and dotted bee trails. Fill removed hives with natural flat short grassy clearings; preserve surrounding rocks and flowers. Keep the wooden shelter structure at middle right of the field but make it empty. Right sidebar starts exactly at 70 percent of screen width; top HUD spans full width and is 11.5 percent screen height. The field should be open with room for three rows of four beehives added at runtime. No characters, no hives, no UI text, no symbols or badges. This is a clean empty scene and UI frame only. Preserve as much of the reference artwork as possible.

## Prompt do atlas

Use case: background-extraction / stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE game sprite atlas, square 1024x1024, transparent RGBA background, exact regular 4 column by 4 row grid of 256x256 cells. Image 1 is the visual style and subject reference. Recreate these sixteen isolated objects in the SAME polished warm hand-painted casual game style, rich golden yellow, dimensional shaded wood, dark soft outlines. Each object centered within its own cell, entirely contained with at least 18px transparent padding, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no environment. Row 1 left to right: (1) golden yellow wooden BEEHIVE with dark brown sloping wooden roof, seen front and slightly from above exactly like the reference hives, WITHOUT grass or stone base, no plaque; (2) full body friendly BEEKEEPER Bento in cream shirt and trousers, brown boots, wide cream sun hat, warm skin, facing slightly left, like reference; (3) cute flying honey BEE with black stripes and white wings, three-quarter side view facing right; (4) golden HONEYCOMB cluster logo with seven hexagonal cells and a small honey drip. Row 2: (1) small glass HONEY JAR filled with amber honey and gold lid; (2) clicking pointing HAND icon; (3) golden royal CROWN; (4) cylindrical silver HONEY EXTRACTOR with lid and tap. Row 3: (1) blue gardening GLOVE hand; (2) wooden garden WHEELBARROW; (3) dark brown SHOPPING CART icon; (4) two green leaves SPROUT growing from a little brown base. Row 4: (1) cozy small beekeeper COTTAGE icon with terracotta roof; (2) two golden green MOUNTAINS icon like sign from reference; (3) warm golden glowing LIGHTBULB tip icon; (4) small wide blank dark wooden PLAQUE with two nails, no digits or text. Transparency must be real alpha, including between all objects. Preserve consistent light from upper left. Perfect rigid grid for programmatic sprite clipping. This is production game artwork, no UI mockup, no screen layout.

O gerador retornou 1254 × 1254; as regiões do atlas foram mapeadas às dimensões reais, preservando o PNG original e seu canal alpha.
